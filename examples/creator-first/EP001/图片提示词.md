# EP001 图片提示词

## IMG-JIANGCHEN-SHEET · 江晨角色板

- 用途：锁定身份、体态和本集常服；供分镜关键帧做 identity reference。
- 参考：无外部参考；三视图必须保持同一脸型、身高比例和服装细节。

### 可复制提示词
> Full-body character sheet of a lean young East Asian man with short cropped black hair, a long face, high brow ridge and slightly deep-set eyes, front, three-quarter and back views on one shared ground line. Straight shoulders and restrained alert posture. Olive-green stand-collar service dress buttoned to the top, straight trousers, black leather boots, visible cotton-twill weave and lightly worn elbows. Soft neutral key light from upper left, consistent anatomy and facial identity across all views, plain warm-grey background, realistic Chinese motion-comic production design, no text, no logo, no watermark.

Olive-green stand-collar officer service dress with plain shoulder boards, washed-pale cuffs, straight trousers and black leather shoes. Calm approachable expression with fatigue held around the brow, consistent identity across all views, soft neutral studio light, plain warm-grey background, realistic Chinese motion-comic production design, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## IMG-OFFICE-PLATE · 两处办公室场景板

- 用途：同页锁定副团长办公室与江晨工位的年代感和固定空间锚点。
- 参考：无外部参考；两区共享旧机关材质；画面之间不共享家具位置。

### 可复制提示词
> Two-panel empty interior location sheet for a period Chinese regiment office. Panel A: a dark-brown glass-covered wooden desk, blue-grey file-box wall and soft daylight from a large window on frame left, with duty sheets beneath the glass and an old tea ring at the edge. Panel B: a dusty old desktop computer, dark-green metal filing cabinets, faded red honours board and cold fluorescent light with a faint green cast, with fabric-front speakers on the cramped desk. Eye-level production plates, fixed architectural anchors, worn practical materials, no people, no modern furniture, no brand logos, no watermark.

## IMG-PHONE-SYSTEM · 手机系统状态板

- 用途：锁定同一手机的熄屏、授予清单和倒计时三个状态。
- 参考：无外部参考；三个状态保持裂纹、磕角、尺寸和相机角度完全一致。

### 可复制提示词
> Three-state prop sheet of a vertical black slab phone with a dented silver frame and a lower-right radial glass crack, identical three-quarter angle and scale in every panel. State one: black inactive screen with one soft reflection. State two: black screen with the exact centered cold-white monospaced Chinese text 已授予：演唱　导演　乐器演奏　伴奏素材《亮剑》×1. State three: the same exact list plus a compact top-right countdown reading 剩余 4 天 23:59:58. Crisp legible hierarchy, neutral studio light, plain warm-grey background, no hands, no brand logo, no watermark.
